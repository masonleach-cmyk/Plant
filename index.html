    Equations in this app

    A(z) ∂θ/∂t = ∂/∂z [ A(z) K(h) (∂h/∂z − 1) ] − A(z) S(z,t)
    θ(h) = θr + (θs − θr) [1 + (α|h|)^n]^(−m), m = 1 − 1/n
    Se = (θ − θr) / (θs − θr)
    K(h) = Ks · Se^½ · [1 − (1 − Se^(1/m))^m]²
    S = ET · a(h) · w(z) / ∫ w A dz, a(h) = 1 above h₃, linear to 0 at h₄
    Full after draining: h(z) = −(L − z)

    z is depth below the soil surface (down is positive), so gravity appears as the −1 inside the flux. Units are cm and days internally; water depths are shown in mm.
